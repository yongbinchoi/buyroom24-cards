Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

Fridgemates Publisher ("the App") is used only by the owner of the TikTok account fridgemates.tv.

What we collect

When the account owner authorizes the App through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives an access token, a refresh token and the TikTok open ID, plus basic profile info (display name) and the account's posting options. We collect nothing from any other person.

How we use it

Only to upload the owner's own videos and check their publishing status. Tokens are stored on the owner's own computer, never on this website, and are not shared, sold or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when the owner revokes access in TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Apps and services) or asks us to delete them.
